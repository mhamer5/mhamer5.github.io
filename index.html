About Me

I am an entomological researcher, with a particular interest in myrmecology, alongside insect diversity, faunistics, as well as taxonomy and evolution. Currently based at the University of Hong Kong as a PhD canditate within the Insect Biodiversity and Biogeography Laboratory.
